==================================================
FAQ
==================================================

Add FAQs:

Do I need my own vehicle?

No. FreightSync also accepts applications from drivers looking to connect with participating vehicle owners.

What types of vehicles do you support?

Cargo vans, Sprinter vans, box trucks, straight trucks, hotshots, pickups, semi trucks, and fleets.

Does FreightSync guarantee loads?

No. FreightSync provides technology, dispatch support, load sourcing assistance, and transportation network resources, but freight availability and revenue cannot be guaranteed.

Does FreightSync take a percentage of every load I run?

No. Percentage-based FreightSync fees apply only according to the customer's selected service agreement and FreightSync-managed or FreightSync-dispatched freight.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no cancellation fee.

Is there a contract?

Customers must accept the appropriate FreightSync service agreement, but the plans should not be marketed as requiring a long-term commitment unless an individual fleet agreement specifically states otherwise.
