---
name: Transportation news expectations
description: Editorial relevance and licensing constraints for FreightSync's public blog.
---

The user wants the blog automatically kept relevant with transportation news: fuel costs and disruptions, new transportation developments, and autonomous vehicles. Its layout and colors should match the homepage.

**Why:** The user identified the static blog and its inconsistent presentation as problems.

**How to apply:** Keep current transportation coverage distinct from evergreen FreightSync guides, with publisher attribution and real dates. Preserve the homepage's visual identity when changing the blog.

Do not use Google News RSS as a commercial-site news backend without appropriate permission, or treat public access as a license to republish full wire stories.

**Why:** The Google News RSS document explicitly limits use to personal, non-commercial feed readers. AP wire access and full-story republication require appropriate licensing.

**How to apply:** Check a prospective feed's reuse terms; prefer linked publisher headlines, and verify licensed access before adding wire content. Never present generated or evergreen text as live reporting.
